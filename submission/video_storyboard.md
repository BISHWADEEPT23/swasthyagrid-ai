## Scene 1: The Invisible Supply Chain Crisis
- **Timestamp**: `0:00 - 0:30` (Duration: 30s)
- **Visual Setup**:
  - Dramatic stock footage or stylized animation of a rural health clinic at night, rain falling outside, doctors looking concerned over empty medicine shelves.
  - Cut to split-screen showing Clinic A with zero stock, and Clinic B 18 km away with stacked boxes of IV fluids.
- **On-Screen Text Overlays**:
  - `THE TRAGEDY: Supplies exist. The roads are open. But the intelligence is missing.`
  - `Over 40% of rural clinics face stockouts during localized outbreaks.`
- **Voiceover Script**:
  > *"Across the developing world, Primary Health Centres are the front lines of healthcare. Yet when seasonal outbreaks hit, rural clinics run out of basic, life-saving medicines like IV fluids and antibiotics.*  
  >  
  > *Not because supplies don't exist in the country—but because public health logistics systems are fragmented, reactive, and completely blind to accelerating demand.*  
  >  
  > *While one clinic runs out, another clinic eighteen kilometers away sits on a twenty-five-day surplus."*
- **Audio / Sound Design**:
  - Somber, low synth drone; distant ambient rain sound; transitions to sharp, rising digital pulse.
